Changelog

What changed in JARVIS, newest first. If something here does not work for you, tell us in the support server.

5.10.0 Dashboard API

  • Transcript link fixed: the link in the DM and in the log now stays valid even if someone presses the Transcript button again (before, a new press made the old link expire).
  • AI limits for your own key: servers that add their own AI key can set how often people may ask, answers per minute and per day, and the longest reply (/ai limits or the dashboard).
  • Tickets: every ticket type counts on its own, so Support and Promotion both start at 0001 (channel names like promotion-0001, support-0001).
  • Transcripts are now stored safely with the rest of your data, so the Open transcript link keeps working after the bot restarts or updates.
  • AI chat: with no channel set it answers when you ping it, in any channel. Pick channels to let it chat freely there. /ai channel only:True keeps it silent everywhere else.
  • Web dashboard: change most settings with clicks. Log in with Discord, pick your server. There is a demo that needs no login.
  • New premium look for the level-up card, with a glowing avatar and a level badge. A long name no longer covers the level.
  • Horror memes: /random type:Meme subreddit:Horror mix (or r/horrormemes, r/creepy, r/LiminalSpace).
  • /role action:time shows how long a timed role has left, and /role action:list shows all timed roles in the server.
  • /setup serverstats makes live counter channels (members, bots, boosts) from a command, not only the dashboard.
  • Fixed an error when a deleted message had no known author (it broke /snipe).
  • Upload welcome, goodbye, ticket and level-card pictures from the dashboard (file or link).
  • The Open transcript button now stays in the DM after you rate a ticket.
  • Antinuke: 100 trusted people, and a check that tells you what is missing so it really works.
  • New look for the invites and messages cards.
  • Warnings: automatic timeout, kick or ban after a number of warnings, set from the dashboard.
  • Scam link rule in automod: fake Nitro, fake Steam and look-alike Discord links.
  • Server stats channels (members, people, bots, online, boosts) created and updated by the bot.
  • Leaderboards for levels, messages and invites share one cleaner look with a podium.
  • Ticket numbers (open, closed, first reply time, rating, top staff) on the dashboard.
  • The dashboard can change most settings: welcome and goodbye, logs, levels with reward roles, automod with banned words, auto replies, AI chat and feature channels, plus an AI help button.
  • Tickets are fully controlled from it (staff roles, ticket types, panel text, limits, posting the panel). Antinuke and anti-raid too, with presets and a lockdown button.
  • The saved welcome or goodbye banner can now be removed from the dashboard.
  • Closed tickets can show an Open transcript button that opens the chat in your browser, with nothing to download.
  • New dashboard pages: birthdays, verification, invite rewards, shop, sticky messages and more log settings.
  • The AI no longer mentions the support server or invite link unless you ask for them.
  • Dashboard: new pages Giveaways, Role panels and Timed roles.
  • Cards: new premium rank card and a welcome picture card with the member's picture and name.
  • Join message: 3 first steps and an Open Dashboard button when the bot is added.
  • Safety: rate limits and protection against request floods.

5.9.0 AI safety, more GIFs, simpler tickets

AI safety

  • The AI no longer shows its instructions, keys or tokens. Tricks like "debug mode" or "ignore previous rules" get a short refusal.
  • Every reply is checked for leaks before it is sent, also when it is hidden in base64, hex or reversed text. User names are cleaned so nobody can fake a message.
  • /ai persona and /ai teach refuse encoded or "decode this" tricks.
  • The AI now looks up the real commands that match a question, so it gives the exact syntax.

Fun

  • Memes come from ten subreddits with a seen list and a "Random mix" option, so they repeat much less.
  • 20 new /interact actions, 61 in total.

Tickets

  • /ticket setup remove_images:true removes the panel and ticket pictures.
  • Advanced subcommands are marked "Advanced:" so the basics are easy to find.

5.8.0 Wizard, backups, sticky, memes

Easier setup

  • /setup wizard is a guided setup in five steps: welcome channel, logs, member role, protection (automod and antinuke), levels and backup. Only menus and buttons, nothing to type.
  • /setup panel shows even more (prefix, backup, sticky messages) and has buttons for the guided setup, the prefix and a backup.

Server backup

  • /setup backup saves your roles, categories and channels with their permissions.
  • Restore adds back only what is missing. Nothing that exists is deleted or changed.
  • Optional daily auto-backup. The last 5 are kept.

Sticky messages

  • /setup sticky keeps a message at the bottom of a channel, as plain text or in a box with title and colour. Great for rules or "how to ask for help".

Fun

  • /random gives a meme (from seven places), a joke, a fun fact, a cat or a dog, with an "Another one" button. !meme works too. It replaces /joke and /fact.

Emoji stealer, tickets and welcome

  • Pictures up to 10 MB are now accepted and shrunk to fit Discord's emoji and sticker limits.
  • The ticket form can be edited: /ticket questions without a type changes it for everyone, and q1:off opens tickets without any form.
  • When someone adds JARVIS they get a thank-you DM. If their DMs are closed, the message is posted in the server instead.

5.7.0 Emoji stealer, /channel, simpler AI

Emoji and sticker stealer

  • /emoji add copies emojis into your server. Paste one or many emojis from any server, an image link, or upload a file.
  • /emoji sticker copies a sticker from a message link or from a file.
  • Faster: reply to any message with !steal and its emojis and stickers are added.
  • Also /emoji big (full size picture), /emoji list and /emoji remove. You need the Manage Expressions permission.

/channel

  • One command for lock, unlock, lockall, unlockall, hide and show.
  • lockall locks every text channel (or only one category). unlockall opens only the channels it locked, so channels you locked on purpose stay locked.
  • !lock and !unlock still work.

AI

  • The AI now talks only in the channels you choose with /ai channel. Nowhere else, not even when someone pings it. No channel chosen means the AI is off in your server.

Better lists and fixes

  • Invite and message profiles show bars, avatars, how many invited people are still in the server, the next reward and recent numbers.
  • Emoji swaps no longer change the emojis you type in /embed, /say, custom commands, auto replies and welcome messages.
  • /antinuke limit without options lists all limits.
  • /lock and /unlock moved into /channel.

5.6.0 GIF fix, new help

  • Anime reaction GIFs now use four GIF sites, so one failing site no longer means no GIF.
  • /help has a cleaner category list with counts, and /help command:ban shows how to use one command.

5.5.0 Reactions, welcome DM, leaderboards

A fun and polish update.

Anime reactions

  • Use /interact or just type .kiss @someone, .hug, .pat, .love, .slap, .cuddle, .poke, .bite, .highfive, .wave and more than 30 others like .cry, .dance, .blush and .laugh.
  • The GIFs are fetched live from a large GIF library, so there are thousands of them and the same one rarely shows twice. They are all high quality and made for this.
  • The person you targeted gets a button like Kiss back. Only they can press it.
  • The footer shows how many times you did it to each other ("Kiss #3 between you two") and which anime the GIF is from.
  • Actions that need a person (like kiss) ask you to pick one. Actions like cry or dance work alone and with someone.

Welcome message in DM

  • When someone adds JARVIS to their server, they get a thank-you DM: the prefix, how to see all commands, a quick start and buttons for the Support server and the Website.
  • If you gave the bot its own server profile (name, picture, banner), the DM uses that. Otherwise the normal JARVIS look.
  • If you add the bot without being able to view the audit log, the server owner gets the DM instead.

Leaderboards

  • Every row has a progress bar compared to the leader, the server icon is on top, and there is a dropdown to switch between today, last 7 days, last 30 days and all time.
  • Messages and invites leaderboards use the same style. The automatic daily, weekly and monthly boards show the server icon too.

Other

  • /rate was removed to make room for /interact (Discord allows only 100 slash commands per bot).

5.4.0 Logs, leaderboards, safety

  • Antinuke is now fully adjustable: /antinuke limit sets how many channel deletes, role deletes, bans and more are allowed, in how many seconds, and whether the person is banned, kicked, stripped of roles or timed out.
  • Message logs show every message of a bulk delete (plus a text file) and a preview of deleted images.
  • Ticket transcripts come as a readable text file and a chat preview, not only a download.
  • Daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards can post themselves: /setup leaderboard.
  • Roles for a limited time: /role add duration:7d. Leave it empty for a permanent role.
  • Switch the AI off in as many channels or categories as you like: /ai ignore.

5.3.0 Levels

  • /rank is now a picture card with your avatar, rank, level and XP bar. Pick your own colour with /rank color:#ff7a00.
  • /leaderboard has pages and time ranges: today, this week, this month or all time.
  • New /levels command for admins: XP per message, cooldown, minimum message length, voice XP, level rewards, role and channel multipliers, a temporary double XP boost, roles and channels that give no XP, and give / remove / set / reset for members.
  • Voice XP: members earn XP per minute in voice (off by default, needs at least two people, muted or deafened members get nothing).
  • Level reward roles can now stack or replace each other, and /levels sync fixes the roles of everyone at once.
  • /levelrole is gone. Use /levels addreward, removereward and rewards.

5.2.0 Tickets, alerts, giveaways

  • Tickets: questions before a ticket opens, several panels with their own types, a close request the member can answer with a button, 1 to 5 star rating by DM after closing, transfer, open ticket list, blocklist and settings for the channel name, staff ping and who can close.
  • /ticket stats shows average first reply time, ratings and the top staff.
  • YouTube and Twitch alerts: /setup youtube and /setup twitch post new videos and live streams, with an optional role ping and your own text. /setup notifications lists, tests and removes them.
  • Giveaways: set the time like 2h or 1d12h, post in any channel, set requirements (level, messages, invites, account age, time in the server), quick /giveaway drop where the first click wins, plus edit, pause, cancel and list.
  • Winners get a DM, and a reroll never picks someone who already won.

5.1.0 Invites, messages, antinuke

  • Invite tracker: /invites shows who invited whom, how many joined, left or were fake, a leaderboard by day, week, month or all time, invite codes, bonus invites and role rewards. Welcome texts can use {inviter} and {invites}.
  • Message stats: /messages shows a member, the whole server, channels and daily activity. /leaderboard can rank messages, levels or invites.
  • Antinuke: if someone deletes channels or roles in a rush, they are punished and the deleted channels and roles are put back. Channels and roles they created are removed. /antinuke preset picks light, standard or strict, and the owner gets a DM when it triggers.
  • Anti-raid: /antiraid lockdown kicks every new joiner for a set time.
  • /ping moved into /about.

5.0.0 Big update

  • Logs: message edits and deletes, joins and leaves, role and channel changes, bans and voice activity, each to its own channel (/setup logs).
  • Automod presets (light, standard, strict) with one click, plus a starter bad-word list.
  • Custom commands: /tag, also usable with the prefix.
  • Giveaways: see who joined, remove someone, extra entries and required or blocked roles.
  • Role panels with up to 10 roles (/rolepanel), a join-to-create voice panel (/voice interface) and auto reactions in a channel (/setup autoreact).
